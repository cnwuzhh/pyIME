under Apache License 2.0. See japanese-LICENSE.txt and japanese-README.txt.
Stellar River changes the schema display name, defaults to Japanese rather
than ASCII mode, and selects Japanese font fallbacks. Dictionary contents
are distributed unmodified, including their original attribution headers.
The Rime engine compiles these dictionaries locally on first deployment.
This input scheme does not bundle or run Google's Mozc conversion engine.
